> 2.3 What do we mean by Sequential vs Parallel Think about exploring business opportunities like running a sophisticated computer simulation. In machine learning, methods like Monte Carlo use ”particles” that evolve or samples to understand complex, uncertain situations - similar to how entrepreneurs might test multiple business hypotheses simultaneously. Some use multiple parallel particles and this is the image we develop on. Just as Monte Carlo methods maintain several possible scenarios to estimate probabilities, modern entrepreneurs like Moderna run parallel experiments rather than testing ideas sequentially. This isn’t just about trying multiple things at once - it’s about maintaining a ”population” of possibilities that can interact and evolve together, much like how genetic algorithms in machine learning combine and refine multiple solutions. When facing high uncertainty, whether in business or computation, parallel exploration helps you understand the full landscape of possibilities and adapt more effectively than step-by-step testing would allow. The math behind sampling algorithms validates what successful entrepreneurs have learned through practice: sometimes the best way to handle uncertainty is to explore multiple paths simultaneously.
